# Stock–bond reversal and window dressing — study note

**Question.** Do SPY and TLT show within-month return reversal or cross-month continuation, and what happens when calendar observations are converted into mechanical trading rules?

The prepared report explores six variations and an ensemble over an approximately 2004–2025 historical sample. It groups returns into the first 15 trading days, final five trading days and the following month's first five trading days. The reported regressions and P&L are research outputs, not independently reproduced returns.

## Outstanding verification

- Distinguish **summed log returns** from investable, compounded portfolio equity; recheck annualized returns, Sharpe, Calmar and capital allocation.
- Verify calendar bucketing, exact trade-entry times and that each signal is observable before the position begins; check missing and incomplete months.
- Investigate overlap and simultaneous legs, whether capital is double counted, and treatment of dividends, shorting, financing, commissions and bid–ask spreads.
- The ensemble components were chosen after results were observed; quantify selection/data-snooping bias and assess a genuine holdout.
- Treat explanations involving institutional rebalancing, window dressing, pension flows and OPEX as proposed mechanisms unless actual flows are observed.
- Verify the statistical claims against the original spreadsheet and source data before presenting the strategies as validated.
